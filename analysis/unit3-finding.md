# Task 3.4 Memo to the dashboard team

To the team that owns the viewings dashboard,

The dashboard you published two weeks ago reports that about half of all viewings end with the renter not showing up and leadership froze the quarterly agent commission payout because of it. We checked that number against the viewing records and it doesn't seem accurate. Out of 200 viewings, 12 were renter no-shows, which is 6%. Another 22 were cancelled for other reasons (the agent couldn't make it, the property was withdrawn or the renter cancelled) and 10 are marked as disputed but those 10 did take place. Counting every one of those as a failure still only gets you to 44 out of 200, or 22%. The other 156 viewings went ahead normally. The senior agent who said the dashboard didn't match what they see day to day was right.

The records are fine and the problem is in the query behind the dashboard. Every viewing has a field for why it didn't happen and for the 166 viewings that went ahead that field is blank, which is how it should be. The dashboard filters on that field in a way that looks like it covers every viewing but any record where the field is blank gets skipped without any error or warnings. That means every viewing that went ahead was left out before the rate was worked out so the rate came from the cancellations that were left. Thats how a 6% problem ends up looking like half. Theres also a second problem in how the query is built. It gives a calculated value a name and then tries to use that name before the database has created it. On its own that just causes an error, but the usual patch is to write the same calculation out twice, and the two copies can drift apart later without anyone noticing.

We recommend lifting the freeze and paying the agent commissions as normal. The freeze was based on a failure rate of about half and the real rate is 6%, so there's no reason left to hold the payout. If any commissions are tied to the 10 disputed viewings, those can be settled through the usual dispute process on their own. Until the query is fixed please dont use the dashboard for payout decisions and check its numbers agaist a simple count of the records before trusting it again. Anything else that was decided from it in the last two weeks is worth a second look too. The corrected queries are in the queries/unit3 folder for whoever looks after the dashboard.

Trust and Data team

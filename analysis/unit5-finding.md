# Task 5.4 Recommendations for the leadership team

To the leadership team,

Our last memo named the properties with the most renter no-shows. Since then we've looked at who those properties are and we also found two properties that have never had a single viewing. These two groups have different problems so we're recommending a different action for each.

## Properties where renters book but don't show up

Property 2 (Property_02 in Danang) had 3 no-shows out of 16 viewings. Property 3 (Property_03 in Haiphong) had 2 out of 13. A normal rate for a busy property on our platform is 4.8% and property 2 is close to 4 times that. Both rent for about 3,200 a month which puts them with the six most expensive listings on the platform. Those six had 7 of the platforms 12 no shows even thouhg they only had 75 of the 200 viewings. Renters are booking these places so interest isn't the issue. The issue is renters not turning up once they've booked.

We recommend that for properties 2 and 3 the agent confirms every viewing with the renter the day before. If the renter dont confirm the slot gets offered to someone else. We run this for the next quarter and compare their no-show rate against the 4.8%. If it works it would make sense to do the same for the rest of the six high-rent listings. Property 4 (Property_04 in Saigon) also had 1 no show but out of only 6 viewings so we keep watching it for now.

None of this should affect agent pay. Our records dont show which agent handled each viewing so a no-show can't fairly be pinned on anyone.

## Properties nobody has booked

Property 14 (Property_14 in Saigon at 651 a month) and property 15 (Property_15 in Hue at 1,343 a month) had no viewings at all in 2023. Both are marked available and both are cheaper than most listings on the platform. Property 14 has been listed since April 2022 so its not a new listing. Nobody is booking these places at all. Either renters arent finding them or something about the listings is putting people off.

We recommend someone on the listings team goes through both listings this month. They should check that each one shows up in search results, including when renters filter by amenity. They should also contact both landlords to confirm the places are still available. Any listing that isnt really available should come off the platform. Going forward we add a monthly check that flags any available listing with no viewings in 60 days so this gets caught in weeks instead of a year.

## Why the two groups get different actions

The fixes dont carry over. A day-before confirmation only helps a property thats getting bookings so it would do nothing for 14 and 15. Checking whether a listing shows up in search wouldnt help 2 and 3 either since renters are already finding and booking them.

The numbers behind all of this are small. 3 no-shows at property 2 could still be bad luck so the quarter of confirmations is as much a test as a fix.

Trust and Data team
